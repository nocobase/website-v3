## NocoBase

### Tagline

> AI + NocoBase. Build reliable business systems.

### Definition

> NocoBase is the open-source platform for building, running, and evolving business systems with AI. Agents write the code and do the work, NocoBase provides the foundation and governance, and people make the decisions.

### Standard description

> NocoBase is the open-source platform for building, running, and evolving business systems with AI. Core is the application foundation. It provides the building blocks and development standards that every business system shares, and agents build on it to meet each company's own needs. Studio is the governance platform where teams and agents develop together. Agents divide up the work and keep it moving, while people stay on top of progress and make the key decisions. NocoBase turns AI's ability to build software into an organization's ability to deliver reliable business systems, release after release.

## Why NocoBase

### In one line

> AI writes code fast. Building reliable business systems is still hard.

### Problems and solutions

Your team is already developing with AI or vibe coding. Code gets written fast, but new problems come with it.

- **Every project rebuilds the foundation**
  - Even with AI writing the code, permissions, workflow engines, and approvals are hard to design from scratch. They are full of edge cases and take a lot of work to maintain.
  - Core provides ready-made building blocks, and NocoBase keeps maintaining and upgrading them. Agents build only the business logic, so there is far less code to write and verify.
- **Architecture drifts out of control**
  - Switch developers, models, or even sessions, and the AI may improvise differently each time. The system gets harder and harder to maintain.
  - Core provides one project structure and one set of development standards, so the system evolves steadily for years on a consistent architecture.
- **Parallel work outpaces human review**
  - You juggle several projects and tasks, switching between terminals and chats, while agents hand back more and more changes. There is more than anyone can review, but letting go feels too risky.
  - Studio coordinates one agent team that moves many projects and tasks forward at once, with progress clear at a glance. When a person needs to step in, agents attach reports, checklists, and screenshots, so people decide with the evidence in hand.
- **Know-how is hard to capture and share**
  - Requirement context, business documents, and lessons learned are scattered across chat sessions on different computers. When people or agents change, they are hard to find.
  - Studio captures methods and experience in skills, the knowledge base, and project memory, so a new person or agent can pick up where the last one left off.

## NocoBase Core

### Tagline

> Let agents focus on your business logic. The foundation is ready.

### Definition

> NocoBase Core is the open-source application foundation for business systems built and run with AI. Permissions, workflows, files, and other building blocks come ready to use, so agents build only what is specific to your business, all on one consistent architecture.

### Standard description

> NocoBase Core is the open-source application foundation for business systems built and run with AI. It provides the building blocks every business system needs, including data modeling, permissions, workflows, files, and AI integration, along with a consistent project structure and development standards. Agents use these ready-made building blocks and build only what is specific to your business. Each project has far less code to write and verify, and the system evolves steadily for years on one consistent architecture.

### Key features

- **Proven building blocks, ready to use**
  - Core provides 20+ building blocks, including permissions, workflows, files, and notifications, so agents can focus on business logic.
- **One architecture, one set of standards**
  - Core provides project scaffolding, architecture standards, and development skills, so the architecture stays consistent and under control as people and models change.
- **You own your apps and data**
  - Core is free and open source. Your business code and data stay in your own repositories and on your own servers, and you choose your own models and agents.

## NocoBase Studio

### Tagline

> Agents keep the work moving. Your team stays in control.

### Definition

> NocoBase Studio is the governance platform for teams building with agents. It brings Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode together into one agent team that moves tasks forward on its own. Progress is clear at a glance, and people make the key decisions.

### Standard description

> NocoBase Studio is the governance platform for teams building with agents. Connect Claude Code, Codex, and other agents on your team's computers and servers, and they form one agent team. A coordinator agent assigns design, development, testing, and review, and moves multiple projects forward on its own. You can see at a glance where every task stands and what comes next. Methods and experience are captured in skills, the knowledge base, and project memory, so a new person or agent can pick up where the last one left off. At every key point, agents attach reports and screenshots, and a person makes the decision.

### Key features

- **An agent team that keeps work moving**
  - Connect agents across many machines and schedule them centrally. They move many projects and tasks forward in parallel, around the clock.
- **Progress at a glance**
  - Project progress, task activity, agent workload, and model usage are all clearly visible.
- **Decisions backed by evidence**
  - When a decision needs a person, agents attach detailed reports, checklists, screenshots, and recommendations, so even business experts who don't code can make the call.
- **Know-how stays with the team**
  - Skills hold how work gets done, the knowledge base holds business documents, and project memory holds what agents learn on their own. When people or agents change, the next one picks up where the last one left off.

## NocoBase Solution

### Tagline

> Open-source business apps. AI-driven, ready out of the box.

### Definition

> NocoBase Solution is a line of agent-driven business apps built on Core. Agents do most of the work, and people make only the key decisions.

### Standard description

> NocoBase Solution is a line of agent-driven business apps built on Core. Traditional business software depends on people to record data, route work, and handle every step by hand. In NocoBase Solution, agents do most of that work: researching customers, drafting emails, reconciling asset records, handling support tickets, and finding SEO and GEO opportunities, acting on them, and tracking the results. When something needs a decision, the agent hands it to the person responsible, with detailed supporting evidence, for the final call. Solution apps are free and open source. Install and use them as they are, or have a coding agent tailor them to your business.

### Key features

- **Agents take the initiative**
  - Each app comes with built-in business agents that follow your rules and proactively take on most of the work, such as checking refunds and handling tickets.
- **People own the key decisions**
  - Agents show the evidence behind their work, and key decisions are always made by a person. Each agent inherits the permissions of the person it works for, so data stays secure.
- **Easy to customize**
  - Every app is built on NocoBase Core, with its consistent architecture and reliable foundation, so you can have a coding agent customize it.
- **Free, open source, ready to use**
  - Every Solution app is free and open source, runs on your own servers, and works with the model of your choice.

## Open source and self-hosting

> Core and Solution are free and open source. Studio is available in the commercial editions.

- **Free and open source**
  - Core and every Solution app are free, open source, and ready for production.
- **Self-hosted**
  - Core, Solution, and Studio all run on your own servers. Your code and data belong to you.
- **Your choice of models and agents**
  - No vendor lock-in. Works with Claude Code, Codex, and other coding tools, and with a wide range of models.
- **Apps run independently**
  - Once released, your business apps run on their own. Day-to-day business requests never go through Studio.

## FAQ

- **How is NocoBase different from vibe coding?**
  - Vibe coding makes building software much faster. But once a project involves complex business logic, many people, and years of iteration, AI-generated code alone isn't enough. You also need a reliable application foundation, consistent development standards, and sound governance. NocoBase doesn't replace vibe coding. It builds on it, adding the foundation and governance.
    - **Building blocks**
      - Without NocoBase: AI generates permissions, workflows, approvals, and other foundational modules from scratch for every project. That is costly, and hard to make reliable.
      - With NocoBase: Core provides ready-made building blocks, and agents build only the business logic.
    - **Architecture standards**
      - Without NocoBase: Switch developers, models, or sessions, and the AI may improvise. The architecture gradually drifts.
      - With NocoBase: Core provides one project structure, development standards, and development skills, so the system evolves steadily for years on a consistent architecture.
    - **Governance**
      - Without NocoBase: Agents hand back more and more changes, more than anyone can review, so they often get accepted as they are. Problems pile up.
      - With NocoBase: Studio coordinates multiple agents through design, development, testing, and review. Key decisions go to a person, with reports and screenshots as evidence.
    - **Know-how**
      - Without NocoBase: Requirement context, project documents, and lessons learned are scattered across individual chat sessions.
      - With NocoBase: Studio captures methods and experience in skills, the knowledge base, and project memory, so a new person or agent can pick up where the last one left off.
