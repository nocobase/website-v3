---
title: "Can I use NocoBase without knowing how to code?"
---

You can participate in application development by describing business needs in natural language, asking an agent to implement them, and trying the result. For customer management, for example, specify which details to store, which customers sales staff can see, and what managers can do. The agent implements the features; you confirm the business rules and accept the result.

In a configured Studio environment, business users can submit requests, answer agent questions, and verify features without first learning to code or setting up a development environment. See [Collaborate with Studio](../getting-started/collaborate-with-studio.md).

Your team still needs someone responsible for environments, account authorization, testing and reviews, deployment, and ongoing maintenance. Agents can assist, but a responsible person must supply the necessary information and confirm results. Administrators can follow the [setup overview](../studio/setup/index.md). To try Core yourself, start with [Develop your first app](../getting-started/develop-your-first-app.md).
