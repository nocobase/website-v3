## Complete your first walkthrough

Read [What is NocoBase 3](what-is-nocobase.md) to understand the application foundation, development governance, and business apps.

- Creating an app yourself: follow [Develop your first app](develop-your-first-app.md) to create, start, and develop customer management, then check role permissions before refining it.
- Joining a Studio team: go directly to [Collaborate with Studio](collaborate-with-studio.md). If the team environment is not ready, an administrator starts with [Installation and configuration](../studio/setup/index.md).

Each walkthrough lists its own prerequisites. You do not need to read every manual first.

## Develop and extend applications

Once the app runs, use the [Application development overview](../core/developing-apps/overview.md) to understand the parts of a feature, and [Project structure](../core/developing-apps/project-structure.md) to locate files. The development manual covers pages and interactions, services and APIs, data and permissions, background work, CLI commands, and testing.

For common capabilities such as authentication, files, and notifications, consult [Using plugins](../core/using-plugins/overview.md). To maintain functionality independently or reuse it across applications, see [Developing plugins](../core/extension-development/when-to-write-a-plugin.md). Consult the [Framework](../core/framework/overview.md) when you need runtime, container, HTTP, or database internals.

## Use and manage Studio

- Members: start with [Raising requests](../studio/use/raise-a-request.md) and [Sessions](../studio/use/sessions.md), then consult [Progress](../studio/use/follow-progress.md), [Answering questions](../studio/use/answer.md), and [Acceptance](../studio/use/accept.md).
- Project owners: use [Settings](../studio/project/settings.md), [Knowledge base](../studio/project/knowledge-base.md), [Project memory](../studio/project/project-memory.md), and [Progress](../studio/project/progress.md), followed by [Checks and review](../studio/project/checks-and-review.md) and [Releases](../studio/release/overview.md).
- Administrators: complete [Installation](../studio/setup/index.md), then manage [Agents](../studio/configure/agents.md), [Runtimes](../studio/configure/runtimes.md), [Skills](../studio/project/skills.md), [Usage](../studio/project/usage.md), [Members and roles](../studio/admin/members-and-roles.md), and [Upgrades and backups](../studio/admin/upgrade-and-backup.md).

Studio's reference section contains statuses, permissions, Agent execution rules, commands, and configuration.

## Follow a complete project

[Build reliable business systems](../guides/build-reliable-enterprise-apps.md) follows an asset-management application through environment setup, design, device management, requests and approvals, roles, acceptance, and deployment. It then introduces Studio collaboration, shared knowledge, and releases.

Use this route for sustained practice across a whole project. If you have completed the quickstart, continue from the stage that matches your experience and consult the manuals for specific mechanisms.

## Deploy, maintain, and look up details

Before sharing an application with users, read [Choose a deployment method](../core/deployment/choose-a-deployment-method.md), then [Publish application releases](../core/deployment/publish-app-release.md). For databases, consult [Compatibility](../core/reference/compatibility.md) and [Database configuration](../core/reference/database-configuration.md).

Look up Core [CLI commands](../core/reference/cli.md) and [Configuration](../core/reference/configuration.md), or Studio [CLI commands](../studio/reference/cli.md), [Runner commands](../studio/reference/runner.md), and [Configuration](../studio/reference/config.md) when needed.
