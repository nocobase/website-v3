NocoBase 3 is used to develop and run business applications. Pages, data structures, and business rules live in your own code repository and are developed by people working with agents. Core supplies the foundation; Studio organizes team development, checks, and delivery.

- **[Core](../core/index.md)** is the open-source application foundation for business systems built and run with AI. Permissions, workflows, files, and other building blocks come ready to use, so agents build only what is specific to your business, all on one consistent architecture.
- **[Studio](../studio/index.md)** is the governance platform for people and agents building software together. It brings Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode into one agent team that moves issues forward on its own. Progress is clear at a glance, and people make the key decisions.
- **Solution** is a line of agent-driven business apps built on Core, such as NocoCRM and NocoITAM. Agents do most of the work, and people make only the key decisions. Install and use them as they are, or have an agent tailor them to your business.

Studio is provided in the commercial edition.

## Development agents and business agents

Development agents use coding tools such as Claude Code and Codex to implement and verify requirements in the application repository. You can work with them locally, or use Studio to coordinate execution Agents through a Lead Agent. Business users then use the released application.

Business agents work inside a running application: researching customers, drafting emails, or checking asset records. Core’s Agents plugin connects them to business capabilities under user roles and permissions, with decisions handed to the responsible person. Development agents change the application; business agents use its business capabilities.

## Running and deploying applications

Core, Solution, and Studio all run on your own servers, and your code and data belong to you. You choose your own models and agents, with no vendor lock-in, and Claude Code, Codex, and other coding tools and a wide range of models all work. Once released, your business apps run on their own, and day-to-day business requests never go through Studio.

Follow [Develop your first app](develop-your-first-app.md) to create and refine a business feature, or [Collaborate with Studio](collaborate-with-studio.md) to follow team delivery. If you have used NocoBase 2, see [NocoBase 2 and NocoBase 3](../faq/from-v2.md).
