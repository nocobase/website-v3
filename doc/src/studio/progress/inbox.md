## How the inbox and My Tasks divide the work

| | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **My Tasks** | Decisions waiting for you on tasks: acceptance, questions to answer, failed runs, and tasks handed to you. See [My Tasks](my-tasks.md). |
| **Inbox** | Comments, @ mentions, and assignments from teammates, plus system items that are not tasks, such as proposed changes to the knowledge base and notices that a runtime needs an upgrade. |

The inbox icon in the top bar opens the inbox.

## Inbox views

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every message. |
| **To decide** | Non-task items that need your decision, such as whether to adopt a proposed change to the knowledge base. |
| **Notifications** | Messages you only need to know about. |

Press <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd> to move up and down the list. Items end on their own once they are handled; marking as read and deleting affect only your own inbox.

## Reminders

When something new is waiting for you:

- A count appears next to **My Tasks** and **My sessions** in the sidebar, in the top bar, and in the browser tab title.
- If the sound reminder is on, you hear a chime. You are not reminded about the task or session you are already looking at.

Adjust these under **Account settings › Preferences**:

- **Sound reminder**: on or off.
- **Desktop notifications**: off by default. When you turn them on, the browser asks for notification permission. If the browser blocks it, allow notifications in the browser's site settings.
- **Push notifications**: reminders reach your phone or another computer even when the Studio page is not open. Each browser subscribes separately.

> [!NOTE] Subscribe to push on a phone
> On Android, open Studio in Chrome and subscribe directly. iPhone and iPad need iOS 16.4 or later: first use **Share › Add to Home Screen** in Safari, then open Studio from the icon on the home screen and subscribe there. Push needs Studio to be served over HTTPS, and an administrator must have set up push.

## Subscribe to a task

Under **More properties** on the task page, **Get notified** lists the people who receive notifications for this task. Select **Subscribe** or **Unsubscribe** to change whether you receive them.

To keep a task in front of you, follow it with the star: followed tasks are listed first in **My Tasks**.
