### 🎉 New Features

- **[Server]** Configure named application mailers once for password reset, invitations and email notifications while retaining existing sender configurations. ([#11284](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11284))
- **[Caching]** Keep custom cache drivers isolated between applications while allowing registration before first use. ([#11302](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11302))

### 🚀 Improvements

- **[Interface]**
  - Share conversation headers across pages and panels, with optional related-work details and list-first navigation. ([#11285](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11285))
  - The agent sessions page and the side panel share one workspace that adapts to the space it has, so they look and work the same. ([#11312](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11312))
- **[Database]** Applications and plugins can recognize duplicate-key errors consistently across supported databases without treating unrelated SQLite constraints as duplicates. ([#11298](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11298))
- **[App foundation]** Keep App Foundation results across restarts and update them in the background on refresh or kernel changes, with compact statistics and clear historical states. ([#11300](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11300))
- **[Agents]** Explain why Runner conversations are waiting and allow withdrawing an unclaimed turn before choosing another Agent. ([#11303](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11303))
- **[App templates]** Move theme settings into the account menu in both application templates while preserving saved preferences. ([#11309](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11309))

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **[Mail]** Allow IMAP/SMTP users to move, archive and delete the selected folder copy safely, with explanations when an action is unavailable. ([#10895](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/10895))
- **[Files]** Center images in file preview dialogs. ([#11292](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11292))
- **[App foundation]** Preserve database model associations through proven local callbacks and query accessors, and reject malformed request header names. ([#11293](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11293))
- **[Interface]**
  - Show the last two characters of Chinese, Japanese and Korean names in default text avatars, ignoring spaces. ([#11294](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11294))
  - Conversation details open inside the conversation and close when you press outside them, on desktop and phone. ([#11304](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11304))
- **[Agents]** Let applications admit agent runs under their executing user's permissions and let runs change only their own comments from the current run. ([#11307](https://github.com/nocobase/nocobase/pull/11307))
