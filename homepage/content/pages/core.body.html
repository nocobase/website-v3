With 30+ ready-made building blocks for permissions, auth, workflows, files, and AI, build business logic right away with your coding tools or scale into team collaboration with Studio.

基础能力Building blocks

权限、工作流引擎、审批、文件存储等底层模块复杂度高、对可靠性要求最高，不由 AI 从零生成，由 NocoBase 持续维护和升级。Agent 只写业务部分，测试和安全审查也集中在新写的业务上。Permissions, workflows, approvals, and file storage are complex and demand the highest reliability—so they are never rewritten from scratch by AI.
