社区版、专业版和企业版如何选择？How do I choose between Community, Professional, and Enterprise?

个人和小团队可以直接免费起步：应用底座（Core）的全部基础能力和全部开源应用开源免费，工作台（Studio）社区版免费使用（含两种内置工作流程），可从 Core 独立起步或直接部署 Studio。Individuals and small teams can start free: every Core building block and all open-source apps are free and open source, and Studio Community Edition is free to use (with two built-in workflows)—start directly with Core or deploy Studio.

一个团队需要规范分工与治理，选专业版：增加多角色流程（业务与技术双重验收）与自定义流程、部署审批、企业 SSO、成本控制，以及高级审批、信创数据库与一次部署服务，技术支持首次响应不超过 12 小时。Choose Professional when a team needs governance: it adds multi-role workflows with dual sign-off and custom workflows, deployment approvals, enterprise SSO, cost controls, advanced approvals, one deployment service, and 12-hour response support.

多团队协作或关键业务系统，选企业版：增加质量管理、自动回归与问题闭环、跨环境发布，以及集群高可用、多租户、多应用、多门户、审计日志和离线交付，包含 3 天部署、培训和实施服务，优先支持首次响应不超过 4 小时。Choose Enterprise for multi-team delivery and mission-critical systems: it adds quality management, automated regression with closed-loop fixes, cross-environment promotions, HA clustering, multi-tenant/multi-app/multi-portal architecture, audit logs, air-gapped delivery, 3 days of onboarding, and 4-hour priority support.

社区版可以用于生产环境吗？Can I use Community in production?

可以。社区版包含完整的 Core 底座、工作台（Studio）的基础功能和所有开源应用，可以直接用于生产环境和商业交付。Yes. Community includes all of Core, Studio’s core features, and every open-source app, free for production and client delivery.

商业版授权的有效期是多久？How long does a commercial license last?

永久有效。商业版一次付费，没有年费，也不按席位收费，已购版本可以一直使用。It never expires. Commercial editions are a one-time purchase with no annual fees and no per-seat charges.

1 年升级与技术支持到期后怎么办？What happens when the first year of upgrades and support ends?

系统照常运行。想继续获得新版本和技术支持，可以单独购买升级和支持服务，且无需连续购买。Everything keeps running. For new releases and support, you can buy upgrades and support separately, and you don’t have to renew every year.

商业版是否私有部署？Are the commercial editions self-hosted?

是的。工作台、Agent 和业务应用都部署在你自己的服务器上，支持纯内网。业务应用发布后独立运行，日常业务请求不经过工作台。Yes. Studio, your agents, and your apps all run on your own servers, including air-gapped networks. Once released, apps run on their own without Studio in the request path.

可以用专业版或企业版为客户开发应用吗？Can I build apps for my clients with Professional or Enterprise?

可以。你基于专业版或企业版开发的业务应用，可以出售给任意数量的客户，无需向 NocoBase 支付额外费用。但不能把专业版或企业版本身分发给未获授权的公司。Yes. You can sell the apps you build with Professional or Enterprise to any number of clients, with no extra fees to NocoBase. You can’t redistribute Professional or Enterprise itself to companies that don’t hold a license.

Studio 需要单独购买吗？Do I need to buy Studio separately?

不需要。Studio 社区版已经免费包含在社区版里，个人和小团队可以直接部署使用；多角色与自定义流程、部署审批、企业 SSO、成本控制，以及质量管理、自动回归与问题闭环和跨环境发布等进阶功能，分别包含在专业版和企业版中。No. Studio Community Edition is already free to use in Community. Team governance, custom workflows, and enterprise quality/release features are included directly in Professional and Enterprise.

可以把商业版 Studio 部署给客户使用吗？Can I deploy commercial Studio for my clients?

不能。商业版（专业版、企业版）Studio 仅限获得授权的公司使用。你可以把用 Studio 开发的业务应用交付给客户，或让客户自行部署 Studio 社区版，但商业版 Studio 本身不能分发给未获授权的公司。No. Commercial Studio (Pro/Enterprise) may only be used by the licensed company. You can deliver the business apps you build to clients or have them self-host Studio Community Edition.

NocoBase 2 商业版用户需要重新购买吗？Do NocoBase 2 commercial license holders need to buy again?

只要 NocoBase 2 专业版或企业版还在升级有效期内，就可以直接使用 NocoBase 3，不用重新购买。标准版不包含在内。2.x 和 3.x 会长期并行维护。As long as your NocoBase 2 Professional or Enterprise license is still within its upgrade period, you can use NocoBase 3 without buying again. This does not include the Standard edition. Both 2.x and 3.x are maintained in parallel.
