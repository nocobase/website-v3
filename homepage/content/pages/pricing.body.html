常见问题FAQ

专业版和企业版如何选择？How do I choose between Professional and Enterprise?

用 Studio 开发和交付业务系统，选专业版：包含 Studio 的全部核心能力和一次部署服务，技术支持首次响应不超过 12 小时。Choose Professional to build and ship business systems with Studio. It includes all of Studio’s core capabilities and one deployment by the NocoBase team, with a first response to support requests within 12 hours.

业务系统需要集群高可用、多租户、审计日志或离线交付，或者开发过程需要质量计划、部署审批和成本控制，选企业版：包含 3 天部署、培训和实施服务，技术支持首次响应不超过 4 小时。Choose Enterprise if your systems need clustering and high availability, multi-tenancy, audit logs, or air-gapped delivery, or if development needs quality plans, deployment approval, and cost controls. It includes 3 days of deployment, training, and implementation, and a first response within 4 hours.

社区版可以用于生产环境吗？Can I use Community in production?

可以。社区版包含完整的 Core 和所有 Solution 应用，可以用于生产和商业交付。你的 Coding Agent 装上 NocoBase 的开发技能就能开发，测试、发布和运维由团队自己负责。Yes. Community includes all of Core plus every Solution app, free for production and client delivery. Add NocoBase’s development skills to your coding agent and start building. Your team handles testing, releases, and operations.

商业版授权的有效期是多久？How long does a commercial license last?

永久有效。商业版一次付费，没有年费，已购版本可以一直使用。It never expires. Commercial editions are a one-time purchase with no annual fees.

1 年升级与技术支持到期后怎么办？What happens when the first year of upgrades and support ends?

系统照常运行。想继续获得新版本和技术支持，可以单独购买升级和支持服务，且无需连续购买。Everything keeps running. For new releases and support, you can buy upgrades and support separately, and you don’t have to renew every year.

商业版是否私有部署？Are the commercial editions self-hosted?

是的。Studio、Agent 和业务应用都部署在你自己的服务器上。业务应用发布后独立运行，日常业务请求不经过 Studio。Yes. Studio, your agents, and your apps all run on your own servers. Once released, your apps run on their own, and day-to-day business requests never go through Studio.

可以用专业版或企业版为客户开发应用吗？Can I build apps for my clients with Professional or Enterprise?

可以。你基于专业版或企业版开发的业务应用，可以出售给任意数量的客户，无需向 NocoBase 支付额外费用。但不能把专业版或企业版本身分发给未获授权的公司。Yes. You can sell the apps you build with Professional or Enterprise to any number of clients, with no extra fees to NocoBase. You can’t redistribute Professional or Enterprise itself to companies that don’t hold a license.

Studio 是否可以单独购买？Can I buy Studio on its own?

不能。Studio 包含在专业版和企业版中。No. Studio comes with Professional and Enterprise.

可以把 Studio 部署给客户使用吗？Can I deploy Studio for my clients?

不能。Studio 仅限获得授权的公司使用。你可以把用 Studio 开发的业务应用交付给客户，但 Studio 本身不能部署给客户使用。No. Studio may only be used by the licensed company. You can deliver the apps you build with it to your clients, but you can’t deploy Studio itself for them.

NocoBase 2 商业版用户需要重新购买吗？Do NocoBase 2 commercial license holders need to buy again?

只要 NocoBase 2 专业版或企业版还在升级有效期内，就可以直接使用 NocoBase 3，不用重新购买。标准版不包含在内。2.x 和 3.x 会长期并行维护。As long as your NocoBase 2 Professional or Enterprise license is still within its upgrade period, you can use NocoBase 3 without buying again. This does not include the Standard edition. Both 2.x and 3.x are maintained in parallel.
