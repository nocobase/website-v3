怎么把 Excel 里的任务导入 Acme？

在问：有一个 200 行的项目计划表，想整体导入，保留负责人和截止日期。

可以整体导入：在项目里点「导入」，选择 Excel 文件，确认每一列对应的字段，负责人和截止日期都会带过来。完整步骤见教程《从 Excel 导入任务到 Acme》。

引用：帮助中心《导入数据》

导入后负责人一栏是空的

在问：表里写的是中文姓名，导入后负责人没有对上。

负责人按成员的显示名匹配。先在「成员」里确认显示名和表里一致，再重新导入；对不上的行会在导入预览里标出来。

引用：帮助中心《成员与显示名》

How do I import my tasks from Excel?

Asking: A 200-row project plan, imported in one go, keeping owners and due dates.

Yes. In the project, click Import, choose the Excel file and confirm the field for each column; owners and due dates come along. The full steps are in the tutorial “Import your tasks from Excel into Acme”.

References: Help center: Importing data

Owners are empty after the import

Asking: The sheet has people’s names, and the owners didn’t match after the import.

Owners are matched by members’ display names. Check that the names in Members match the sheet, then import again; rows that don’t match are flagged in the import preview.

References: Help center: Members and display names
