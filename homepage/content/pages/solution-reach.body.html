每日分析Daily Analysis

每天，Every day, 自动找出值得做的事。NocoReach finds what’s worth doing.

每天在你设定的时间，NocoReach 按分析规则检查所有数据的变化，由 AI 逐条判断是否值得做，并写明依据。Every day at a time you choose, NocoReach checks your data for changes against your analysis rules, and AI decides change by change whether it is worth acting on, with the evidence.

  • 先看整体The big picture first

  • 值得做的，一行一件One line for each thing worth doing

    交给 Agent，或者忽略。没有新发现时，不凑数。Hand it off or dismiss it. A day with nothing new adds nothing.
