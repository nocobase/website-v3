NocoBase Core开源应用底座Open-source app foundation

让 Agent 专心写业务，通用底座已经就绪。Let agents focus on your domain. The foundation is ready.

NocoBase Core 是开发和运行企业业务系统的开源底座。认证、权限、工作流、通知、文件、AI 员工等通用能力以插件提供，Agent 直接使用；在此之上的业务，比如页面、数据表、谁能看什么、单据怎么流转，都是你仓库里的源码，可评审、可回退。底座随版本独立升级，业务不受影响。NocoBase Core is the open-source foundation for building and running business systems. Auth, permissions, workflows, notifications, files, and AI employees ship as plugins that agents use directly. What you build on top—pages, tables, who can see what, how records move—is source code in your repo, reviewable and revertible. Core upgrades on its own without disturbing your business.

优势Why Core

同样让 AI 开发业务系统，Same AI, 起点更高，交付更稳。higher starting point, steadier delivery.

AI 写代码已经很快，难的是写出来的系统能不能上线、能不能放心交给业务用、明年还能不能改。Core 把这些交给底座来保证。AI already writes code fast. The hard part is whether the system can go live, whether the business can trust it, and whether it can still change next year. Core makes the foundation responsible for that.

01

Agent 只写业务Agents write only your business

认证、权限、工作流、审批、通知、文件、AI 员工已经做好并经过验证。Agent 不必每个项目重造一遍，代码量少、出错少、交付快。Auth, permissions, workflows, approvals, notifications, files, and AI employees are built and proven. Agents don’t rebuild them per project—less code, fewer bugs, faster delivery.

02

业务都是你的源码Your business is your source

页面、数据表，以及具体的权限规则和业务流程，都在你的仓库里。每次改动可评审、可测试、可回退，复杂规则也写得出来。Pages, tables, and your actual permission rules and business flows live in your repo. Every change can be reviewed, tested, and reverted—and complex rules are no problem.

03

底座独立升级The foundation upgrades on its own

Core 的能力以插件提供，业务代码不改底座。新版本带变更说明和配套的开发指引，升级平稳、可控。Core’s capabilities ship as plugins and your code never patches them. Each release comes with notes and matching coding guides, so upgrades are smooth and predictable.

04

身份与访问Identity & access每一次访问都有明确的身份，权限决定能做什么Every request has an identity; permissions decide what it can do

认证Authentication

账号密码、社交账号与 OIDC 单点登录（SSO），会话管理，程序通过 API key 访问；SAML、LDAP 与企业微信、飞书、钉钉登录由商业版提供。Password sign-in, registration and recovery, sessions, and API keys for programs. Customize the sign-in page and extend to OIDC and other enterprise or third-party sign-in.

权限Permissions

按岗位组合页面、操作与数据范围的权限集，支持共同范围、共享规则、限制规则和授权来源检查，人和 Agent 使用同一套权限。Permission sets combine pages, actions, and data scopes by role, with shared scopes, sharing rules, restriction rules, and an access inspector—the same rules for people and agents.

数据Data应用自己的数据，加上已有系统的数据Your app’s data, plus the data you already have

数据源Data sources

默认使用 SQLite，也可换用其他数据库；接入已有外部数据库，明确字段对应、读取范围与访问权限，在业务页面直接使用。SQLite by default, or another database of your choice. Connect existing external databases—mapping fields, read scope, and access—and use them right in your pages.

文件Files

上传、预览、下载业务附件并关联到记录；默认本地存储，也支持 S3 兼容存储，服务端管理配置与凭据。Upload, preview, and download attachments linked to records. Local storage by default or any S3-compatible store, with credentials managed on the server.

流程与协作Process & collaboration让业务按规则推进，把消息送到对的人Move work by the rules and reach the right people

工作流Workflows

用状态机描述阶段、分支与条件；保存等待状态，处理重试与补救；按需呈现阶段栏、流程图和办理历史。State machines define stages, branches, and conditions; waits, retries, and recovery are handled; show stage bars, flowcharts, and history as you need.

审批Approvals商业版Commercial

条件分级、会签、或签、加签与转交；申请、待办与进度可嵌入业务详情。由审批插件提供，需要相应授权。Conditional tiers, joint and any-one sign-off, add-signer and handover; requests, to-dos, and progress embedded in records. Provided by the approval plugin under a commercial license.

通知Notifications

站内信、邮件（SMTP 或 Resend）、飞书与钉钉群消息，各渠道独立保存投递结果。In-app messages, email (SMTP or Resend), and Feishu or DingTalk group bots, with delivery results kept per channel.

邮件Email

员工关联自己的邮箱（IMAP/SMTP、Gmail、Microsoft 365），在客户和订单页面阅读、回复与编写，使用签名和模板。Staff connect their own mailboxes (IMAP/SMTP, Gmail, Microsoft 365) and read, reply, and compose from customer and order pages, with signatures and templates.

定时任务Scheduled jobs

按时间或周期提醒待办、生成报表、同步数据，保留执行时间、耗时与状态，可暂停和恢复。Remind, report, and sync on a schedule, with run time, duration, and status recorded—pause and resume any time.

模板打印Template printing

提供 Office 模板与字段，由 Agent 实现生成、预览或下载；可生成 DOCX，PDF 需服务端转换工具。Bring an Office template and fields; agents build generation, preview, or download. DOCX out of the box; PDF needs a server-side converter.

AI 知识库AI knowledge base

用空间、文件夹、文章和文件组织企业知识，按岗位控制阅读和维护；AI 在允许的范围内检索并引用来源。Organize knowledge in spaces, folders, articles, and files with role-based reading and editing. AI searches within what it’s allowed to see and cites its sources.

业务 AgentBusiness Agents

业务 Agent 和同事，Business agents and your team 遵守同一套规则。follow the same rules.

系统上线后，业务 Agent 在其中整理客户资料、分类工单、准备入职事项。它们和人使用同一套角色、权限、审批与操作记录。Once live, business agents organize customer data, triage tickets, and prepare onboarding—using the same roles, permissions, approvals, and audit trail as people.

只看、只改被授权的数据Only the data it’s granted

Agent 和人使用同一套角色与权限。例如，供应商 Agent 只能读取本部门负责的供应商，不能修改准入状态。Agents and people share roles and permissions. A supplier agent reads only its department’s suppliers and can’t change approval status.

关键操作由人批准People approve key actions

审批规则写在工作流里，超出授权的操作交给负责人决定，每次都按同样的方式流转。Approval rules live in workflows. Anything beyond an agent’s authority goes to its owner, the same way every time.

每一步都查得到Every step is traceable

谁做的、改了什么、依据哪条规则，都有操作记录，人和 Agent 的操作一样可以查询与追溯。Who did it, what changed, and which rule applied—recorded and searchable for people and agents alike.

Core 支持应用内 Agent 和外部 Agent：应用内 Agent 在应用提供的环境中运行，外部 Agent 在独立环境中运行并连接应用。具体能做什么，取决于所用的模型、工具与授权。Core supports in-app agents and external agents: in-app agents run inside the app’s environment; external ones run separately and connect to it. What they can do depends on the model, tools, and permissions you give them.
