常见问题FAQ

找伙伴和直接找 NocoBase 有什么区别？Partner or NocoBase directly—what’s the difference?

NocoBase 提供产品、授权和 Launch；伙伴在你所在的地区和行业提供完整交付，包括定制、集成、迁移和长期运维。需要时两边也可以一起参与。NocoBase provides the product, licenses, and Launch. Partners deliver end to end in your region and industry, including customization, integration, migration, and long-term care. Both can be involved when needed.

费用怎么算？How is pricing set?

服务费用由伙伴报价，常见方式有按项目、按模块或按年运维；NocoBase 商业版授权按官方价格，也可以通过伙伴购买。Partners quote their services—per project, per module, or as annual care. NocoBase commercial licenses follow official pricing and can be bought through the partner.

交付的代码归谁？Who owns the delivered code?

归你。业务是你仓库里的源码，通用能力由 NocoBase Core 插件提供；交付范围里会写明代码、Skills、知识库和维护责任。You do. Your business is source code in your repository, with common capabilities provided by NocoBase Core plugins; the scope spells out code, Skills, knowledge, and maintenance responsibilities.

以后能换伙伴，或者自己维护吗？Can we switch partners or maintain it ourselves later?

可以。系统基于标准的 NocoBase Core，方法写在 Skills 里，业务知识在知识库里，换人或自己接手都不用从头了解项目。Yes. The system sits on standard NocoBase Core, the methods are in Skills, and the business knowledge is in the knowledge base—so anyone taking over doesn’t start from scratch.
