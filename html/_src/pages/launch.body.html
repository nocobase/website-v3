NocoBase Launch实战培训与陪跑Training & Coaching

我们的团队陪着你的团队，一起跑通第一个真实需求。Our team works with yours to ship your first real need.

NocoBase Launch 不是照本宣科的理论课，而是围绕真实需求展开的实战陪跑。你们带上当前最想落地的一项业务需求，业务与技术负责人共同参与，NocoBase 团队或伙伴和你们一起完成开发、检查、验收与上线。完成后，你的团队能自己给 Agent 安排需求、判断结果，并把改动发布上线。NocoBase Launch isn’t a lecture—it’s hands-on coaching around a real requirement. Bring the one thing you most want to ship, with your business and tech leads in the room, and NocoBase engineers or a partner will build, check, verify, and release it with you. Afterward, your team can assign work to agents, judge the results, and ship on its own.

常见问题FAQ

Launch 需要先购买商业版吗？Do we need a commercial edition first?

Launch 在 NocoBase Studio 中进行，适合准备采用企业版的团队。具体安排在预约时一起确认。Launch runs in NocoBase Studio, so it suits teams planning to adopt Enterprise. We’ll confirm the details when you book.

需要准备什么？What should we prepare?

一个真实需求、能确认业务规则的业务负责人，以及能处理环境、权限和集成的技术负责人。服务器、模型与 Agent 工具由企业自行选择和承担费用。One real requirement, a business lead who can confirm the rules, and a tech lead who can handle environments, permissions, and integrations. You choose and pay for servers, models, and agent tools.

可以由伙伴来做 Launch 吗？Can a partner run Launch?

可以。NocoBase 团队或伙伴都可以和你们一起完成；项目成果、代码与维护职责在交付范围中明确。Yes. The NocoBase team or a partner can work with you; deliverables, code ownership, and maintenance are spelled out in the scope.

Launch 能帮我们把整个系统做完吗？Will Launch build our whole system?

Launch 的目标是让你的团队学会这套方式，并完成第一个真实需求。系统代建、迁移和复杂集成按项目另行约定。Launch is about your team learning the method and shipping a first real requirement. Building whole systems, migrations, and complex integrations are scoped separately.
