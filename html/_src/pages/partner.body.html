常见问题FAQ

需要先有一支开发团队吗？Do we need a development team first?

不需要很大的团队。懂业务的顾问在 Studio 里就能主导需求和验收；建议至少有一位技术负责人，负责环境、集成和发布把关。Not a large one. A consultant who knows the business can lead requirements and acceptance in Studio; we recommend at least one technical lead for environments, integrations, and release sign-off.

几种合作方式可以同时选吗？Can we combine models?

可以。很多伙伴既销售授权，也提供交付和运维；有行业积累的伙伴还会做自己的行业方案。Yes. Many partners resell licenses and deliver services; those with deep industry experience also build their own solutions.

服务收入需要和 NocoBase 分成吗？Do we share service revenue with NocoBase?

不需要。部署、定制、集成、培训和运维的收入 100% 归你，价格由你自己定。No. Revenue from deployment, customization, integration, training, and ongoing care is 100% yours, at prices you set.

模型和 Agent 工具的费用怎么算？Who pays for models and agent tools?

由你或客户自行选择和承担，NocoBase 不加价。可以用 Claude Code、Codex、OpenCode 等工具，以及任意模型，包括本地部署的模型。You or your client choose and pay for them directly; NocoBase adds no markup. Use Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and any model, including self-hosted ones.

还能用 NocoBase 2 为客户交付吗？Can we still deliver on NocoBase 2?

可以。NocoBase 2 与 NocoBase 3 并行、持续维护。适合在界面里配置的项目可以用 NocoBase 2，需要 AI 开发和复杂业务的项目用 NocoBase 3，伙伴计划同时覆盖两者。Yes. NocoBase 2 is maintained alongside NocoBase 3. Use 2 for projects best configured in the UI and 3 for AI-built, complex ones—the program covers both.

我们开发的行业方案归谁？Who owns the industry solutions we build?

归你。你可以选择开源，也可以按自己的方式商业授权；进入 Solutions 目录和联合推广的方式在合作中约定。You do. Open-source it or license it commercially as you choose; catalog listing and joint promotion are agreed as part of the partnership.
