常见问题FAQ

Professional 和 Enterprise 如何选择？How do I choose between Professional and Enterprise?

一个团队用 Studio 开发，选 Professional：包含一次部署服务，技术支持首次响应不超过 12 小时。Choose Professional if one team builds with Studio. It includes one deployment by the NocoBase team and a first response to support requests within 12 hours.

多个团队协作，或需要跨环境发布、集群高可用、审计日志、离线交付，选 Enterprise：包含 3 天部署、培训和实施服务，技术支持首次响应不超过 4 小时。Choose Enterprise for multiple teams, or if you need multi-environment release, high availability, audit logs, or air-gapped delivery. It includes 3 days of deployment, training, and implementation, and a first response within 4 hours.

Community 可以用于生产环境吗？Can I use Community in production?

可以。Community 包含完整的 Core 和全部 Solution 应用，可以用于生产和商业交付。你的 Coding Agent 装上 NocoBase 的开发技能就能开发，测试、发布和运维由团队自己负责。Yes. Community includes all of Core and every Solution app, free for production and client delivery. Add NocoBase’s development skills to your coding agent and start building. Your team handles testing, releases, and operations.

商业版授权的有效期是多久？How long does a commercial license last?

永久有效。商业版一次付费，没有年费，已购版本可以一直使用。It never expires. Commercial editions are a one-time purchase with no annual fees.

1 年升级与技术支持到期后怎么办？What happens when the first year of upgrades and support ends?

系统照常运行。想继续获得新版本和技术支持，可以单独购买升级和支持服务，且无需连续购买。Everything keeps running. For new releases and support, you can buy upgrades and support separately, and you don’t have to renew every year.

商业版是否私有部署？Are the commercial editions self-hosted?

是的。Studio、Agent 和业务应用都部署在你自己的服务器上。业务应用发布后独立运行，日常业务请求不经过 Studio。Yes. Studio, your agents, and your apps all run on your own servers. Once released, your apps run on their own, and day-to-day business requests never go through Studio.

Studio 是否可以单独购买？Can I buy Studio on its own?

不能。Studio 包含在 Professional 和 Enterprise 中。No. Studio comes with Professional and Enterprise.

NocoBase 2 商业版用户需要重新购买吗？Do NocoBase 2 commercial license holders need to buy again?

只要 NocoBase 2 商业版还在升级有效期内，就可以直接使用 NocoBase 3，不用重新购买。2.x 和 3.x 会长期并行维护。As long as your NocoBase 2 commercial license is still within its upgrade period, you can use NocoBase 3 without buying again. Both 2.x and 3.x are maintained in parallel.
