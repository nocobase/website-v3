常见问题FAQ

购买一次可以用多久？How long can I use it after buying?

永久。商业版是一次性付费的永久授权，没有年费；购买的版本可以一直使用。Forever. Commercial editions are a one-time payment for a perpetual license with no annual fee—you can keep using what you bought.

第一年之后会怎样？What happens after the first year?

软件照常使用，不受影响。价格里包含购买后 1 年的产品升级和技术支持；之后如果还想获得新版本和官方支持，可以联系我们续期。Your software keeps working as before. The price includes 1 year of upgrades and technical support; after that, contact us if you’d like to continue receiving new releases and support.

Professional 和 Enterprise 怎么选？Professional or Enterprise?

一个团队用 Studio 带领 Agent 开发和交付，选 Professional；有多个团队、需要开发、测试、预发、生产多套环境的发布流转，或需要集群、高可用和离线交付，选 Enterprise。Choose Professional if one team uses Studio to build and ship with agents. Choose Enterprise for multiple teams, promotion across dev, test, staging, and production, or clustering, high availability, and offline delivery.

只用开源的 Community 可以吗？Can I just use Community?

可以。Community 包含 NocoBase Core 和全部官方应用，可以用于生产，也可以为客户交付。给你的 Coding Agent 装好 NocoBase 3 的 Skills，就能直接基于 Core 开发；测试、部署和备份由你的团队自己安排。Yes. Community includes NocoBase Core and every official app, for production and client delivery. Install the NocoBase 3 Skills for your coding agent and build on Core directly; your team handles testing, deployment, and backups.

Studio 能单独购买或免费使用吗？Can I get Studio on its own or for free?

Studio 包含在 Professional 和 Enterprise 中，没有单独的免费版本。想先看看效果，可以预约一次实战验证。Studio comes with Professional and Enterprise; there’s no separate free edition. To see it in action first, book a pilot.

还有哪些费用？Are there other costs?

服务器、模型和 Agent 工具（如 Claude Code、Codex）由你自己选择，按各自服务商的价格付费。Studio 里可以看到每次运行的用量，并设置上限。You choose and pay for servers, models, and agent tools (like Claude Code or Codex) at their providers’ prices. Studio shows usage per run and lets you set limits.

商业版也是私有部署吗？Is the commercial edition self-hosted?

是的。Studio、开发用的 Agent 和业务应用都运行在你自己的环境里；业务应用发布后独立运行，日常访问不经过 Studio。Yes. Studio, your coding agents, and your business apps all run in your own environment. Published apps run on their own—daily traffic never goes through Studio.

伙伴可以用哪个版本为客户交付？Which edition can partners deliver with?

任何版本都可以。用 Community 就能为客户开发和交付；客户需要 Studio 的团队协作和受控发布时，再选择商业版。Any of them. Partners can build and deliver with Community, and choose a commercial edition when a client needs Studio’s collaboration and controlled releases.

我在用 NocoBase 2，这里的价格适用吗？I use NocoBase 2. Does this pricing apply?

NocoBase 2 的商业版用户可以直接升级到 NocoBase 3，无需重复购买。两个版本并行、持续维护，你可以按自己的节奏迁移。If you have a NocoBase 2 commercial license, you can upgrade to NocoBase 3 directly—no need to buy again. Both versions are maintained in parallel, so you can move at your own pace.

不确定选哪个版本？Not sure which edition fits?

告诉我们你的团队和要做的系统，我们帮你选；也可以先预约一次实战验证。Tell us about your team and what you’re building and we’ll help you choose—or start with a pilot.
