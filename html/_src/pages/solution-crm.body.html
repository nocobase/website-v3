你的销售团队里，多了一支 7×24 小时的 Agent 团队。Your sales team just gained a 24/7 agent team.

NocoCRM 不是在 CRM 旁边加一个聊天框。它内置 6 个有岗位的销售 Agent，接通邮箱、WhatsApp、网站表单和日历：新线索、客户来信、到期任务一出现就主动跟进；开会前把要点备好，审批前把账算清，每天早上盯住你关注的公司。它们只提建议，关键决定由你来做——而且每个 Agent 的表现都看得见。NocoCRM isn’t a CRM with a chat box bolted on. It comes with six sales agents, each with a job, wired into email, WhatsApp, web forms, and calendars: they follow up the moment a lead arrives, a customer writes, or a task falls due; prep your meetings, do the math before approvals, and watch your accounts every morning. They only suggest—you make the calls—and every agent’s track record is visible.
