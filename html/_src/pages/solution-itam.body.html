Agent 主动核对、追踪、跟进，IT 资产井井有条。Agents reconcile, track, and follow up. IT assets stay in order.

传统资产系统是一本需要人去维护的账。NocoITAM 里，Agent 每天替 IT 干苦活：MDM 一同步就把差异整理成方案，新人入职前把设备和席位配齐，每一笔采购先查库存和闲置，订阅续费前把没人用的席位收回来。每台设备、每个席位、每一笔钱，都有人管、有据可查。Traditional asset tools are a ledger someone has to keep. In NocoITAM, agents do IT’s grunt work every day: they turn every MDM sync into a plan, kit out new hires before day one, check stock and idle assets before every purchase, and reclaim unused seats before renewals. Every device, seat, and dollar has an owner and a record.

采购Purchasing

每一笔采购，Before every purchase, 先看库存、闲置和历史。stock, idle assets, and history come first.

采购单一提交，审批助手就替审批人算一笔账：库存或闲置能不能顶上、同型号以前多少钱、同类申请以前怎么批的、申请人哪里没说清。建议同意、有条件同意、驳回或补充材料，并起草审批意见。The moment a request is submitted, the approval assistant does the math for the approver: can stock or idle assets cover it, what did the same model cost before, how were similar requests decided, what did the requester leave out. It recommends and drafts the comment.

{{SNIP:itamJourney:1120}}

审批助手给出的，不只是“同意”或“驳回”。More than “approve” or “reject.”

  • “建议有条件同意：库存里有 3 台同型号显示器可以先用，只买差的 2 台。”“Approve on conditions: 3 matching monitors are in stock—buy only the 2 still needed.”
  • “建议驳回：这台打印机才用了 20 个月，也没有维修记录，先报修，不要直接销毁。”“Reject: this printer is only 20 months old with no repair history—repair before disposing.”
  • “建议有条件同意：同型号没有库存，但在库还有 3 台可用的替代机型；先确认本季度预算和新人名单再买。”“Approve on conditions: no stock of that model, but 3 alternatives are available—confirm budget and the new-hire list first.”

入职与离职Joiners & Leavers

新人第一天就能开工，New hires work on day one. 离职的人一件不落地收回。Leavers return everything.

入职前，系统按入职配置算好该给什么、库存里哪台能补、哪个席位可以分配、还缺什么；资产管家整批提出，你确认一次就好，缺口直接起草采购单。离职时反过来，设备、配件、席位，再加上停用账号、更换密钥这些系统外的事，一项不漏。Before a start date, NocoITAM works out what the hire needs, which device in stock and which seat can cover it, and what’s missing. The asset steward proposes it all as one batch—confirm once, and gaps get a drafted PO. Leavers work in reverse: devices, accessories, seats, plus off-system steps like disabling accounts and rotating keys, nothing missed.

MDM

MDM 每次同步后，After every MDM sync, 差异自己整理成一份方案。differences sort themselves into a plan.

和 Intune、Jamf Pro 同步后，MDM 跟进 Agent 把需要判断的差异整理好：使用人不一致的交给设备负责人，台账没登记的交给资产管理员，每项附上理由；拿不准的，比如失联 41 天的笔记本，留给人看。在收件箱里勾选，确认后执行。After syncing with Intune or Jamf Pro, the MDM follow-up agent sorts what needs a decision: assignee mismatches go to the device owner, unregistered devices to the asset admin, each with a reason—and anything uncertain, like a laptop silent for 41 days, is left for a person. Tick what you agree with in the inbox and it runs.

  • 不再每周导出两张表手工对No more weekly spreadsheet comparisons
  • 每一处差异都有处理人和理由Every difference has an owner and a reason
  • 执行记录留在设备的历史里What was done stays in the device’s history
