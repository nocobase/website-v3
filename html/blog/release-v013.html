NocoBase 2.2 正式版发布Announcing NocoBase 2.2

NocoBase 2.2 正式版进一步完善 V2 的完整使用路径，带来独立、轻量的 /v/ 前端入口与全新移动端体验。同时升级文件访问与权限机制，并增强评论区块、AI 知识库、工作流及核心插件的 V2 支持。NocoBase 2.2 further completes the V2 experience with a standalone, lightweight /v/ frontend and an all-new mobile experience. It also upgrades file access and permission controls while expanding V2 support across comments, AI knowledge bases, workflows, and core plugins.

NocoBase 2.2-beta 发布Announcing NocoBase 2.2-beta

NocoBase 2.2-beta 版本带来了多工作区、新移动端、新评论区块，同时继续提升 AI 知识库和工作流能力。以及全新的 `/v/` 分支——它会作为面向 V2 的独立前端入口，承接后续的功能演进。NocoBase 2.2-beta brings Multi Portal, a new mobile client, and a new comment block, while continuing to enhance the AI knowledge base and workflow capabilities. It also introduces the brand-new /v/ branch—which will serve as a dedicated front-end entry for V2 and carry forward all future feature development.
