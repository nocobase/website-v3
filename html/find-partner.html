常见问题FAQ

找伙伴和直接找 NocoBase 官方有什么区别？What’s the difference between working with a partner vs. NocoBase directly?

NocoBase 官方提供产品底座、商业授权与 Launch 实战陪跑；伙伴则深入你所在的地区和行业，提供端到端的定制开发、系统集成、数据迁移与长期维保。重点项目也可由原厂与伙伴联合服务。NocoBase provides the platform, commercial licenses, and Launch engagements. Partners provide end-to-end implementation in your region and industry, including custom builds, integrations, migrations, and long-term care. Both can team up when needed.

费用怎么算？How does pricing work?

实施与维保服务由伙伴按项目、模块或年度维保报价；NocoBase 商业版授权执行官网统一价格，可直接向伙伴一并采购。Partners quote implementation and maintenance by project, module, or annual retainer. NocoBase commercial licenses follow official pricing and can be purchased through your partner.

交付的代码归谁？Who owns the delivered code?

完全归你。业务逻辑全部是你仓库里的干净源码，通用能力由 NocoBase Core 插件提供；合同中会明确约定源码、技能、知识库及维保权责。You do. Your business logic lives as clean source code in your repository, powered by NocoBase Core plugins, with code, Skills, knowledge, and support terms clearly defined.

以后能换伙伴，或者转由内部团队维护吗？Can we switch partners or bring maintenance in-house later?

随时可以。系统构建在标准的 NocoBase Core 之上，开发规范沉淀在技能中，业务上下文保存在知识库里，任何团队接手都无需从零摸索。Anytime. Because the system is built on standard NocoBase Core with practices in Skills and context in the knowledge base, your own team or another partner can take over smoothly.
