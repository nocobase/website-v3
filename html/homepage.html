01

底层能力要从零建设Every project rebuilds the foundation

即使交给 AI，权限、工作流引擎、审批等底层模块从零开发，设计难度依然很高，边界情况多，维护工作量大。Even with AI writing the code, permissions, workflow engines, and approvals are hard to design from scratch. They are full of edge cases and take a lot of work to maintain.

02

03

并行任务很多，人确认不过来Parallel work outpaces human review

同时处理多个项目和任务，在多个终端和对话之间切换，Agent 交过来的改动也越来越多，人看不过来，又不敢放手。You juggle several projects and issues, switching between terminals and chats, while agents hand back more and more changes. There is more than anyone can review, but letting go feels too risky.

04

知识和经验难以沉淀和共享Know-how is hard to capture and share

需求背景、业务资料、项目经验散落在不同电脑的不同会话里，换人、换 Agent 就很难找到。Requirement context, business documents, and lessons learned are scattered across chat sessions on different computers. When people or agents change, they are hard to find.

NocoBase 和 Vibe Coding 有什么区别？How is NocoBase different from vibe coding?

Vibe Coding 让软件开发更加高效，但当项目涉及复杂业务、多人协作和长期迭代时，仅靠 AI 生成代码还不够，还需要可靠的应用底座、统一的开发规范和完善的治理。NocoBase 并不是 Vibe Coding 的替代品，而是在它之上补齐底座和治理能力。Vibe coding makes building software much faster. But once a project involves complex business logic, many people, and years of iteration, AI-generated code alone isn’t enough. You also need a reliable application foundation, consistent development standards, and sound governance. NocoBase doesn’t replace vibe coding. It builds on it, adding the foundation and governance.
