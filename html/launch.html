常见问题FAQ

实战验证收费吗？Does the pilot cost anything?

不收费。实战验证适合正在考虑 Professional 或 Enterprise 的团队。预约后我们会先和你聊一聊，一起把需求和参与的人定下来。No. The pilot is for teams considering Professional or Enterprise. After you book, we’ll have a short call to settle the requirement and who joins.

选什么需求来做验证最合适？What requirement should we bring?

选一个你们现在最着急解决的问题。比如还在靠表格和群消息推进的审批，或者几个部门总是对不上的数据。问题越迫切，两天后看到的应用就越能说明这套方法值不值得用。Pick the problem you most urgently need solved, such as approvals still run through spreadsheets and group chats, or data that never matches across departments. The more pressing the problem, the more the app you see two days later tells you whether this approach is worth it.

参加 Launch 需要先买商业版吗？Do we need a commercial license first?

实战验证在签约前进行，不需要先购买。落地实施在签约后开始，Professional 包含 1 天，Enterprise 包含 3 天。Not for the pilot, which happens before you sign. Onboarding starts after you sign: 1 day with Professional, 3 days with Enterprise.

需要准备什么？What do we need to prepare?

实战验证阶段，只需要一个具体需求，以及一位能拍板的业务负责人参加成果会，环境和模型由我们提供。签约后，服务器、大模型与 Coding Agent 由企业自备，按我们提供的部署文档就能自己部署 Studio，遇到问题随时找我们。For the pilot, just one concrete requirement and a business lead who can make decisions at the review; we provide the environment and models. After you sign, you bring your own servers, models, and coding agents and deploy Studio yourselves with our deployment guide; we’re here if you get stuck.

Launch 是帮我们把整套系统包干做完吗？Will Launch build our entire system for us?

Launch 的目标是和你们一起做出第一个应用，在这个过程中把工作方法留在你的团队里，之后你们可以自己带领 Agent 继续开发。整套系统代建、数据迁移或复杂集成，可以按项目另行委托。Launch is about building your first app together and leaving the way of working with your team, so you can keep leading agents on your own. Full turnkey builds, migrations, and complex integrations can be scoped as separate projects.
