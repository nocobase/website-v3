常见问题FAQ

参加 Launch 需要先买商业版吗？Do we need a commercial license first?

Launch 全程在 NocoBase Studio 中进行，面向计划采用商业版的团队。具体安排可在预约时一起确认。Launch runs inside NocoBase Studio and is designed for teams adopting Professional or Enterprise. We’ll align on details when you book.

启动前需要准备什么？What do we need to prepare?

一项真实业务需求、能拍板规则的业务负责人，以及负责环境与权限的技术负责人。服务器、大模型与 Coding Agent 由企业自备。One real requirement, a business lead to make rule calls, and a tech lead to handle environments and access. You bring your own servers, models, and agent tools.

可以由合作伙伴交付 Launch 吗？Can a partner deliver Launch?

可以。NocoBase 官方团队或认证伙伴均可提供陪跑，交付成果、代码归属与后续职责都会在协议中明确约定。Yes. Either the NocoBase team or a certified partner can run Launch, with deliverables and code ownership defined up front.

Launch 是帮我们把整套系统包干做完吗？Will Launch build our entire system for us?

Launch 的核心是“授人以渔”：带你们跑通第一个真实需求，让团队学会自己驾驭 Agent。如需整套系统代建、迁移或复杂集成，可按项目另行委托。Launch focuses on enabling your team while shipping your first real feature. Full turnkey builds, migrations, and complex integrations can be scoped as separate projects.
