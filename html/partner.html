常见问题FAQ

需要先组建一支开发团队吗？Do we need a large development team first?

不需要庞大的开发团队。懂业务的顾问即可在 Studio 中主导需求与验收；建议至少配备一位技术负责人，把关环境、系统集成与上线发布。No. Business consultants can lead scoping and acceptance in Studio. We simply recommend having at least one technical lead to oversee environments, integrations, and release sign-off.

多种合作方式可以同时选吗？Can we combine partnership models?

可以。许多伙伴既分销商业授权，又提供定制交付与年度维保；深耕垂直行业的伙伴还会将经验沉淀为自有行业方案。Yes. Many partners resell licenses while delivering implementation and annual care, and vertical specialists often package their own industry solutions.

服务收入需要和 NocoBase 分成吗？Do we share service revenue with NocoBase?

不需要。部署、定制、集成、培训和运维收入 100% 归你，定价完全自主。No. 100% of your revenue from deployment, customization, integration, training, and maintenance stays with you at whatever prices you set.

模型和 Agent 工具的费用怎么算？How are models and agent tools billed?

由你或客户直接向对应服务商采购，NocoBase 不加收任何差价。可自由接入 Claude Code、Codex、OpenCode 及各类云端或本地私有模型。You or your client pay the providers directly with zero markup from NocoBase. Use Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and any cloud or local model.

还能继续用 NocoBase 2 为客户交付吗？Can we still deliver projects on NocoBase 2?

可以。NocoBase 2 与 NocoBase 3 长期并行维护：适合可视化配置的项目用 2.x，需要 AI 深度开发与复杂逻辑的项目用 3.x，伙伴权益同时覆盖两个版本。Yes. NocoBase 2 is maintained alongside NocoBase 3. Use 2.x for UI-configured apps and 3.x for AI-built, complex systems; the partner program covers both.

我们开发的行业方案知识产权归谁？Who owns the industry solutions we build?

完全归你。你可自行决定开源或商业授权方式；上架官方目录及联合推广事宜在合作协议中具体约定。You do. Open-source or license them commercially on your own terms; catalog listing and co-marketing are arranged together.
