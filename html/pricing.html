常见问题FAQ

买一次可以用多久？How long is the license valid?

永久有效。商业版为一次性买断授权，没有年费，已购版本可一直使用。Forever. Commercial editions are a one-time perpetual license with no annual fees.

满一年之后会怎样？What happens after the first year?

已部署的软件照常运行。首年包含免费版本升级与技术支持；期满后如需继续获取新版本或官方支持，可按需续订。Your software keeps running without interruption. The purchase includes 1 year of updates and support; after that, you can optionally renew to get future releases and support.

Professional 和 Enterprise 怎么选？Professional or Enterprise?

单一团队用 Studio 带领 Agent 开发交付，选 Professional；多团队协作、需要开发/测试/预发/生产多环境流转审批，或需要高可用集群与离线交付，选 Enterprise。Pick Professional for a single team building with Studio. Pick Enterprise for multi-team governance, multi-stage release pipelines, high-availability clustering, or air-gapped deployment.

只用开源的 Community 可以吗？Can I use Community in production?

完全可以。Community 包含完整的 Core 底座与全部官方应用，可用于企业生产和客户商业交付。装上官方 Skills 即可让你的 Coding Agent 直接开发，测试、部署与备份由团队自理。Yes. Community includes the full Core foundation and all official apps, free for production and client delivery. Install the official Skills to build with your own coding agent and CI/CD.

Studio 能单独购买或免费试用吗？Is there a free or standalone version of Studio?

Studio 包含在 Professional 与 Enterprise 中，不提供独立免费版。你可以先在线体验，或预约一次实战验证（Launch）。Studio is included with Professional and Enterprise. You can try it online or book a hands-on pilot first.

还有其他隐性费用吗？Are there any other costs?

没有。服务器、大模型 API 与 Coding Agent（如 Claude Code、Codex）由你自行选购，直接向对应服务商付费；Studio 内置用量与费用统计。None from us. You bring your own servers, LLM APIs, and coding agents at their providers’ standard rates, with usage and cost tracked in Studio.

商业版也是私有部署吗？Are commercial editions self-hosted?

是的。Studio、开发 Agent 与业务应用全部运行在你自己的服务器环境中；业务应用上线后独立运行，日常流量不经过 Studio。Yes. Studio, your agents, and your apps all run on your own infrastructure. Production apps run standalone, so live user traffic never goes through Studio.

合作伙伴可以用哪个版本为客户交付？Which edition can partners use for client delivery?

任一版本均可。直接用免费的 Community 就能为客户开发和交付系统；当客户需要 Studio 协作或企业级增强特性时，再选购商业版。Any edition. Partners can build and deliver client systems on Community for free, or choose a commercial edition when clients need Studio or enterprise features.

NocoBase 2 商业版用户如何升级？I have a NocoBase 2 commercial license. Do I pay again?

NocoBase 2 商业版用户可直接免费获得 NocoBase 3 授权，无需重复购买。2.x 与 3.x 将长期并行维护，你可按自己的节奏平滑演进。No. NocoBase 2 commercial license holders upgrade to NocoBase 3 at no extra cost. Both 2.x and 3.x are actively maintained in parallel.

拿不准选哪个版本？Not sure which edition fits?

聊聊你的团队规模与业务场景，我们帮你评估；也可以先带一个真实需求做一次实战验证。Tell us about your team and project, or bring a real requirement for a hands-on pilot.
