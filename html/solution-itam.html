采购Purchasing

每一笔采购前，Before every purchase, 先查库存、闲置与历史价格。check stock, idle assets, and past prices.

采购单一提交，审批助手自动算好细账：是否有库存或闲置可用、历史单价多少、同类申请往常怎么批、申请说明是否完整，并直接给出审批建议与批注草稿。As soon as a request is submitted, the approval assistant checks whether stock or idle assets can cover it, compares past unit prices and similar requests, flags missing details, and drafts a recommendation and comment.

入职与离职Joiners & Leavers

新人首日即可开工，New hires are ready on day one. 离职资产一件不落收回。Leavers return every item.

入职前，按岗位标准算好所需设备与软件席位，自动匹配库存与闲置，打包生成配置方案；你只需确认，缺口一键起草采购单。离职时反向核销，设备、配件、席位以及停用账号、轮换密钥等系统外事项逐一追踪，不漏一项。Before day one, NocoITAM matches the new hire’s standard kit against stock and idle seats in a single batch. Confirm it, and draft a PO for any gaps in one click. When someone leaves, it tracks devices, accessories, seats, and off-system tasks like revoking accounts and rotating keys until everything is cleared.
