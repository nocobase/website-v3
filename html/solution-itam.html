传统资产系统是一本靠人手动维护的台账。在 NocoITAM 里，日常杂活由 Agent 代劳：MDM 同步后自动整理差异方案，新人入职前配齐设备与软件席位，每笔采购前先查库存和闲置，订阅续费前主动回收闲置账号。每台设备、每个席位、每一笔开支都责任到人、有据可查。Traditional ITAM tools are ledgers you have to maintain by hand. In NocoITAM, agents handle the daily legwork: turning MDM sync diffs into action plans, kitting out new hires before day one, checking stock and idle assets before every purchase, and reclaiming unused seats before renewals. Every device, seat, and dollar has an owner and a clear audit trail.

入职与离职Joiners & Leavers

新人首日即可开工，New hires are ready on day one. 离职资产一件不落收回。Leavers return every item.

入职前，系统按岗位标准算好所需设备与软件席位，自动匹配库存与闲置，打包生成配置方案；你只需确认一次，缺口自动起草采购单。离职时反向核销，设备、配件、席位以及停用账号、轮换密钥等系统外事项逐一追踪，不漏一项。Before day one, NocoITAM matches the new hire’s standard kit against stock and idle seats in a single batch. Confirm once, and any gaps become a draft PO. When someone leaves, it tracks devices, accessories, seats, and off-system tasks like revoking accounts and rotating keys until everything is cleared.
