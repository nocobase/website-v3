每日分析Daily Analysis

每天，Every day, 自动找出值得做的事。NocoReach finds what’s worth doing.

不用等周报，也不用翻十几个后台。每天 09:00，NocoReach 按分析规则检查所有数据的变化，由 AI 逐条判断是否值得做，并写明依据。No waiting for the weekly report, no tour of a dozen dashboards. Every day at 09:00, NocoReach checks your data for changes against your analysis rules, and AI decides change by change whether it is worth acting on, with the evidence.

  1. 01

    规则找出变化Rules find the changes

    内置 44 条分析规则，覆盖搜索、访问与转化、内容、社区、产品关注与使用和竞品。规则可以用一句话调整，比如“搜索点击下降超过 20% 就提醒我”。44 built-in rules cover search, traffic and conversions, content, community, product interest and usage, and competitors. Adjust them in a sentence, such as “Tell me when search clicks drop more than 20%.”

  2. 02

    AI 判断值不值得做AI weighs each change

    结合项目知识逐条判断，结论分三种：建议处理、建议观察、无需处理，并写明理由和依据。With your project knowledge in mind, AI gives each change one of three verdicts (act on it, watch it, or no action needed) with its reasons and evidence.

  3. 03

    写成机会Opportunities, written up

    建议处理的写成机会，等你交给 Agent 或忽略；建议观察的写明判断条件和截止日期，到期再判断。没有新发现时，不凑数。Changes worth acting on become opportunities for you to hand off or dismiss. Changes to watch get a condition and a deadline and are judged again then. A day with nothing new adds nothing.

成果Results

上线之后，效果自动对比。After launch, the impact is compared for you.

改动上线后第 7 天和第 28 天，自动对比改过的页面在上线前后同样天数的搜索点击和浏览量。结论分为上升、下降、无明显变化和数据不足，只描述数据变化，不推断因果。On days 7 and 28 after launch, NocoReach compares each changed page’s search clicks and views with the same number of days before. Each result reads up, down, no clear change, or not enough data. It describes the data and does not claim causes.
