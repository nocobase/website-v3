业务 AgentBusiness agents

Agent 主动工作。Agents take the initiative.

传统业务软件靠人来记录、流转和操作。在 Solution 里，每个应用中内置很多业务 Agent，它们会按照规则主动承担大部分执行工作，比如核查退款、处理工单。Traditional business software depends on people to record data, route work, and handle every step by hand. In NocoBase Solution, each app comes with built-in business agents that follow your rules and proactively take on most of the work, such as checking refunds and handling tickets.

每个 Agent 管什么、什么时候工作、按什么方法做，都写得清清楚楚。Every agent spells out its job, when it works, and how.

  • 事情发生就开始工作：Starts when something happens: 新线索进来、客户来信、任务完成时，对应的 Agent 自动开始工作，也可以按时间定期运行。a new lead, a customer email, or a finished task sets the right agent to work, and agents can also run on a schedule.
  • 按你的规则做事：Follows your rules: 业务指导写明企业的背景和口径，技能写明做事的方法，改了以后，Agent 下次运行就照新的做。business guidance sets out your context and standards, skills set out the method, and the next run follows any change you make.
  • 做了什么都有记录：Keeps a record: 每个 Agent 运行了多少次、哪些被采纳、哪些被忽略，都记录在案。how often each agent ran, and what was accepted or ignored, is all on record.
