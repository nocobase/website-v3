核心差异What’s Different

不是在软件旁边挂个聊天框，Not a chatbot bolted onto software. 而是给团队配齐上岗干活的 Agent。An agent team with real jobs.

多数软件的“加 AI”只是放一个等你提问的侧边栏助手。NocoBase Solutions 里的 Agent 是有明确岗位、触发时机、权限边界与工作记录的团队成员；而且整套软件的源码，也能交给 AI 随时改造。Most “AI features” are just sidebar chatbots waiting for prompts. In NocoBase Solutions, agents have defined roles, triggers, permissions, and track records, and the entire app is open source for coding agents to customize.
